# ⏱️ Hour 01: OS for the AI Era & Getting Started with openEuler
**Pillar:** HCIA-openEuler V2.0 (Operating System & Cloud-Native iSula)  
**Official Material Reference:** Chapter 01 (pp. 1–22) & Chapter 02 (pp. 23–54)  
**Pacing:** 60 Minutes (4 × 15-Minute Active Learning Sprints)

Master the architecture and 5 core functions of openEuler Intelligence (AI for OS), trace the evolution from Unix and Minix to GNU/Linux and openEuler LTS/Innovation releases, walk through openEuler installation and partitioning, and execute local/remote logins and Bash CLI shortcuts.
